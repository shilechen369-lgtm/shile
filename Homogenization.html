Homogenization

2024 · TouchDesigner · Audio-Visual

The struggle of individuals trying to maintain their own thoughts and identities while being pulled into groupthink. The "quagmire" implies that the more individuals try to resist or escape the collective consciousness, the more they become trapped, Mired in the mud of homogenized ideas. This can be seen as a metaphor for social pressure, propaganda, or loss of individuality in a group setting.

Once individuals are involved in the quagmire of homogenous thinking, it becomes increasingly difficult to escape the framework of society.
