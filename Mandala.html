Dance Mandala

2024 · TouchDesigner · Audio-Visual

The purpose of this arwork is to explore and integrate the two very different patterns of Chinese traditional window flowers and Indian mandala, but the same culture, and through them show the unique understanding and expression of "happiness" in different cultures.

It is hoped that viewers can feel the happy resonance across the way when appreciating this work, and realize the common yearning and blessing for happiness in different cultural backgrounds.
