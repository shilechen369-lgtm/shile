Möbius Strip

2024 · TouchDesigner · Audio-Visual

Möbius Band is a generative visual work built upon continuous motion and directional flow. Each branching line emerges from different positions in space, bending and intertwining as it moves, gradually converging toward a shared center. Rather than being forcibly drawn inward, the lines follow their own internal logic of motion, forming a structure that exists between dispersion and unity. The image presents a looping state with no clear beginning or end, echoing the single-sided and infinite nature of the Möbius strip.

Avoiding explicit narrative, the piece relies on pure motion and rhythm to construct a visual experience that oscillates between order and chaos, inviting viewers to perceive the folding of time, direction, and space.
